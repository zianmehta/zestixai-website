Questions

Straight answers

How much do salons actually lose from missed calls?

A salon missing just 8 booking calls a day loses real money over a year, and most of those callers simply book with the next salon that answers. The real cost compounds because one client relationship is often worth years of repeat visits, not just one appointment.

Does this replace my front desk?

No. Most salons start in overflow mode: your team answers first, and the AI only picks up calls that would have gone to voicemail. Nobody's role changes.

Can it book for specific stylists or services?

It collects the client's name, number, preferred stylist or service, and preferred time, then sends it to your booking system or front desk to finalize.

What if a caller asks something it can't answer?

It takes a clear message — name, number, reason — and sends it to your team right away. It's built never to invent an answer and never to hang up on a caller.
