FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Will the AI sound robotic to my customers?

Today's voice AI is conversational and natural — most callers can't tell. We script it around your business, your services, and your tone, and you approve exactly how it sounds before it ever takes a live call. Anything complex gets handed to a human.

What happens to my customer data?

It stays yours. We build on your accounts, you own every conversation and record, and nothing is sold or shared. We'll walk you through exactly where data lives and sign whatever confidentiality terms you need.

How do I know it's actually working?

Every system we build reports real numbers — calls answered, appointments booked, leads responded to, hours saved. You see them at the 30-day checkpoint and every month after. If the numbers aren't there, we fix it or you stop paying.

Do I need to be technical or hire IT staff?

No. We handle setup, integration, and maintenance, and we train your team in plain English. If you can use email and a calendar, you can run everything we build.

How long does it take to go live?

A single automation is typically live in one to two weeks. A full AI receptionist system usually takes two to four weeks including testing on real scenarios. Your written proposal includes the exact timeline.

Am I locked into a contract?

No long-term lock-in. Setup is a one-time fixed fee, ongoing support is month-to-month, and if we part ways you keep your data, your accounts, and your systems.
