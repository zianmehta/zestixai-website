What she handles

Everything that currently goes to voicemail

Built around your business — your hours, your menu or services, your prices, your policies. She never invents an answer, and she never hangs up on a customer.

Reservations & appointments

Collects the name, number, party size and time, repeats it back to confirm, and sends it straight to your team. When a slot is full, she offers the next best times instead of losing the guest.

Takeout & orders

Takes the order item by item, confirms it back with a total, and sends the ticket to your kitchen — or texts the customer your online ordering link.

Catering & big-ticket leads

The calls worth the most money. She captures event type, date, headcount and contact details, and flags it for a same-day callback so a $2,000 party never lands in voicemail.

The usual questions

Hours, location, parking, prices, specials, dietary options, gift cards — answered instantly, correctly, every time, without pulling anyone off the floor.

English, Hindi & Spanish

She detects the caller's language and switches mid-call without missing a beat. Most answering services charge extra for one extra language — or don't offer it at all.

Messages that actually arrive

Anything she can't handle becomes a clean message — name, number, what they wanted — delivered to you immediately, so a human can close the loop.

Questions

Straight answers

Does this replace my staff?

No — and we'd talk you out of it if you asked. We start in overflow mode, so your people answer first and the AI only catches what would have gone to voicemail. It adds capacity during your busiest moments instead of removing people.

What if she can't answer something?

She takes the caller's name, number and reason and sends it to you immediately so a human can call back. She's built never to invent an answer and never to hang up on a caller.

How long does setup take?

Usually a few days. We build her around your information, test her together until you're happy, then point your overflow calls at her. Your existing phone number stays exactly as it is.

What does it cost?
